# Getting help

- **Questions and ideas:** use GitHub Discussions on the relevant repository when
  they are enabled, and read its documentation first.
- **Bugs:** open an issue on the relevant repository, following its issue form.
- **Security problems:** do not open an issue; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Paid support and MapCova Plus:** <https://my.mapcova.com/offers>.
- **Commercial licence and anything else:** <hello@mapcova.com>.

Community support is best effort, with no response time guarantee.
